# Personal walking insights and route suggestions

The first on-device version joins completed or ended-early PausePath walking sessions with available Apple Health **walking workouts**. An overlapping Health workout is omitted when it appears to represent the same PausePath session (at least 70% overlap of the shorter recording and, when both have distance, within 35% of each other). The PausePath recording retains its route identity and optional check-in. Other Health activity and day-wide step totals do not enter this combined walking timeline. Health access remains optional and the combined timeline is never sent to the backend.

Apple's [statistics collection guidance](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/healthkit/executing-statistics-collection-queries) applies to quantity samples, not workouts. The app continues to use HealthKit statistics for daily steps and calories; walking-workout duration is aggregated from the individual workouts so records can be deduplicated against PausePath sessions.

My Path → Insights shows an eight-complete-week series of walking minutes per day, averaged within each week. A least-squares line is the displayed smooth trend; its slope is minutes per day per week. The view needs records in at least two weeks before presenting a slope. The baseline is the first four weeks, the recent comparison is the last four, and the additional-minutes figure compares the observed total with holding the first-four-week average for all 56 days. These are **observed changes**, not effects attributable to PausePath, recovery assessments, or safe-exertion advice. Weeks without recorded sessions count as zero for this workout-based measure; a missing Health permission or missing workout cannot be interpreted as no walking.

The synthetic demo profile uses 12, 13, 14, 15, 16, 17, 18 and 19 minutes per day across eight complete weeks. It produces a 1 minute/day/week slope, a 29.6% baseline-to-recent increase, 868 total minutes, and 112 minutes above a constant 13.5-minute baseline. Turn **Sample walking insights** on in Profile to display this clearly labeled data in Insights and to use it for route ordering. It does not replace or upload personal records.

Route planning first rejects options outside the requested ±25% distance range, missing required facility categories, known slopes above the chosen limit, or mapped rest gaps exceeding the selected maximum. Remaining options are scored against the median distance of up to 12 recently completed walks, excluding walks marked “Too demanding” in an optional check-in. Without such walks, the chosen distance is the target. A provisional option can appear when slope or rest reliability is unknown, but is labeled as needing verification. Confidence is a qualitative indicator of data completeness and history volume, **not** a probability of safety. Existing map directions do not currently supply measured maximum grade, surface, or crossing details, so those stay unknown. The suggestion can be dismissed; preferences change only when the user changes them.

Next steps: add a source for verified per-segment elevation, surface and crossing data; validate deduplication with two real Health-connected devices; check recommendation copy and chart readability with larger text; calibrate scoring with consenting users' feedback. An online preference model is optional and requires a separate privacy and evaluation design. No language model is needed for the current scoring or explanation.
